## AI-Powered Lead Capture & Management Portal

I want to build the existing Lead Capture and Management Portal as an AI-powered sales intelligence and automation platform, rather than a traditional CRM with a chatbot added to it.

The difference is significant:

A traditional CRM stores leads, tracks activities and generates reports.

An AI-powered CRM should identify promising prospects, qualify them, recommend next actions, automate follow-ups, detect buying intent and help sales teams convert more leads into paying customers.

The new AI-powered CRM product that can serve software agencies, consultants, service businesses and eventually other industries.

### The 12 AI capabilities:

### - AI Lead Capture Assistant

    Replace static forms, where appropriate, with conversational AI. It asks relevant questions based on the visitor's requirements, identifies missing information and creates a structured lead.

### - Intelligent Lead Scoring

    Calculate scores using customer fit, engagement, urgency, budget and buying intent. Explain why a lead deserves attention.

### - AI Lead Enrichment

    Use permitted business data sources to identify industry, company size, website, technologies and possible business requirements. Distinguish verified data from AI inferences.

### - AI-Personalized Follow-Ups

    Generate contextual email and WhatsApp drafts based on the lead's industry, prior conversations, interests and pipeline stage.

### - Conversation Intelligence

    Summarize calls, meeting transcripts, emails and chats. Extract requirements, commitments, objections and action items.

### - Opportunity Intelligence

    Analyze opportunity health, detect stalled deals and suggest next steps. Identify potential deal risks from inactivity or unresolved objections.

### - AI Lead Assignment

    Automatically recommend the most suitable sales representative for every incoming lead.

    Instead of assigning leads randomly or manually, AI evaluates the lead's requirements, industry, location, estimated deal value and sales representatives' expertise, availability and performance.

### - AI Sales Copilot

    An intelligent assistant that allows sales representatives and managers to interact with CRM data using natural language.

    Instead of manually searching records, applying filters and generating reports, users can ask questions directly and receive relevant, permission-controlled answers.

### - AI Proposal Assistant

    Automatically generate professional sales proposals from customer requirements and CRM conversations.

    AI analyzes customer inquiries, discovery notes, emails, uploaded requirement documents and opportunity details to generate an editable proposal.

### - AI Revenue Forecasting

    Estimate future sales revenue by analyzing opportunities, pipeline stages, historical performance and customer engagement.

    AI helps sales managers understand how much revenue might close, which deals are at risk and whether the organization is likely to meet its sales targets.

### - AI Duplicate Detection

    Identify duplicate leads arriving through different channels, even when their names, email addresses or company details are slightly different.
    Traditional CRM systems often identify duplicates using exact email or phone matches. AI-assisted detection can also identify likely matches involving spelling variations, company aliases and incomplete contact information.

### - AI Marketing Attribution

    Analyze which marketing channels and campaigns generate qualified leads, opportunities and actual revenue.

    Traditional CRM reporting often focuses on how many leads each channel produces. AI-assisted attribution goes further by examining lead quality, conversion performance, customer journeys and revenue outcomes.

Keep the existing features and add the AI features on top of that. Make the database and API level changes accordingly.
